# Orion shows places and vehicles as published, not people

Orion is growing from a satellite tracker into a layered map of public information: infrastructure, air and sea traffic, and weather. Much of that data can also be used to follow a particular person, by their aircraft, their boat or the cameras they pass. We decided that Orion shows places and vehicles as their sources publish them and builds nothing whose purpose is identifying or following an individual. The owner's reason is that a tool for following people would not be something to publish as open source.

## Consequences

- Any Feature can be selected and shows what its source publishes about it. That is the limit.
- No watchlists of people, no lookups from a vehicle to its owner as a private individual, and no merging of sources to build a profile of a person.
- A request for such a feature is out of scope for this project, not a prioritisation question.
